Drawing on behavioral economics and constructivist social theory, the framework formalizes alignment as jointly influencing world states and human evaluative states. The authors argue alignment should govern long-term preference trajectories, ensuring they remain coherent, reflectively endorsed, and epistemically grounded.
💡 Why It Matters： This challenges the fixed-preference assumption common in alignment research, potentially reshaping AI safety approaches. As AI systems become more persistent and socially embedded, regulating their influence on human value formation becomes central. The framework reframes alignment as a governance problem with implications for future AI design and policy.
Background
Standard alignment treats preferences as static targets to infer and optimize. Constructivist psychology holds that preferences are constructed dynamically through interaction. Control theory provides mathematical tools for regulating evolving systems, which this preprint extends to the co-evolution of human values and AI behavior over time.
Key Details
Technically, preferences are modeled as hierarchical state variables, with a control framework that jointly optimizes system actions and interaction design. Five alignment desiderata are proposed: coherence, reflective endorsement, epistemic grounding, boundedness against manipulation, and empowerment under uncertainty. The theory integrates control theory, psychology, and behavioral economics.

The court confirmed that Google illegally required phone makers to pre-install Google Search and Chrome as a condition for licensing the Google Play Store, paid manufacturers and operators to exclusively pre-install Google Search, and obstructed the development of Android forks by preventing the sale of non-compatible devices. 📊 Key Facts: $4.7 4.34
Background
The European Commission enforces EU antitrust rules and can impose fines up to 10% of a company's annual worldwide turnover. Rulings can be appealed first to the General Court and then to the European Court of Justice. Android is open-source, but Google's proprietary apps and services are bundled to maintain its search and mobile ecosystem dominance. The case began with an EU investigation in 2015, a Statement of Objections in 2016, and the formal decision in 2018.
Key Details
The Commission identified three specific illegal practices: (1) bundling the Play Store license with pre-installation of Google Search and Chrome; (2) granting financial incentives to certain manufacturers and network operators to pre-install only Google Search; and (3) obstructing the development and distribution of Android forks by prohibiting the sale of devices running uncertified versions. Background
The neutral rate, often referred to as r-star or the natural rate of interest, is the real interest rate consistent with full employment and stable inflation. The net effect depends on the magnitude and timing of these opposing forces.
