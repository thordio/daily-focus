Background

Current AI training relies on human-created datasets, and data scarcity is a growing bottleneck as models advance. BigBang's approach automates the creation and refinement of training tasks, offering a step toward self-sustaining improvement cycles.
Key Details
Instead of optimizing individual answers or programs, BigBang optimizes the entire curriculum system, including task generation, distribution, and evaluation. The synthetic data pipeline is calibrated using real research task performance to ensure relevance and difficulty.
