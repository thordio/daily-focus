AgentNAS: LLM-Powered Automatic Search Space Design for NAS

rss ArXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-11 · ⌛ 6 min read
Reason：The paper proposes a novel integration of LLMs and NAS to automate search space design, a significant technical contribution that could influence future AutoML research. Pure AI research, not market-related.
📰 What's New
Researchers propose AgentNAS, a method that uses a large language model to generate a seed architecture and decompose it into a slotted architecture with interchangeable module slots, automatically defining a task-specific search space for conventional neural architecture search without manual design. On 17 tasks from NAS-Bench-360 and Unseen NAS covering classification, regression, segmentation, and multi-label tagging, AgentNAS achieved state-of-the-art on 11 tasks, surpassing task-specific expert designs. Ablation studies show that the LLM-generated seed alone already outperforms published baselines on most tasks, while NAS adds further gains through combinatorial recombination across slots, a mode of search that independent LLM samples cannot replicate. The approach proved robust across three LLMs of different capability levels.
💡 Why It Matters： This work bridges the gap between manual search space engineering and fully automated NAS, reducing the need for domain expertise when applying NAS to new tasks. The demonstrated complementary division of labor between LLMs and NAS could influence future AutoML systems to dynamically generate search spaces rather than relying on fixed, human-designed ones. As LLMs become more capable, such hybrid approaches may enable rapid adaptation of deep learning models across diverse domains without extensive manual intervention.
Background
Neural Architecture Search (NAS) automates the design of neural network architectures but typically operates within manually defined search spaces that require expert knowledge and must be re-engineered for each new task. Large Language Models (LLMs) have been used to generate neural architectures directly, but lack the optimization capabilities of NAS. The key challenge addressed is how to optimally combine the open-ended generation of LLMs with the structured search of NAS to automate the entire architecture design process.
Key Details
AgentNAS operates in three phases: an LLM produces a seed architecture, decomposes it into a slotted architecture with named, interchangeable module slots, and then a conventional NAS algorithm explores the resulting bounded search space. The slotted architecture allows combinatorial recombination of modules, which ablation studies show provides gains that independently sampled LLM architectures cannot achieve. The method was evaluated on 17 diverse tasks, and the LLM seed alone surpassed published baselines in the majority of cases, with NAS contributing additional improvements in most scenarios.

Key Details

Overthinking Technique Amplifies Reasoning to Extract Model Secrets

rss ArXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-11 · ⌛ 4 min read
Reason：Introduces a novel technique for extracting hidden information from language models by amplifying reasoning weights, with strong implications for AI safety and auditing. Preprint status limits slightly from breakthrough.
📰 What's New
Researchers at ICML 2026 introduced a method called 'overthinking' that amplifies reasoning task vectors in language models to surface hidden information. The technique creates an overthinking model using the formula θ_Oα = θ_M + α(θ_R - θ_M) with α > 1, revealing secrets up to 10 times more frequently than the original reasoning model. They tested it across four experimental settings, on models ranging from 2 billion to 32 billion parameters.
💡 Why It Matters： The method provides a new tool for AI safety audits, allowing developers to probe for subtle misalignments or unintended behaviors that standard black-box testing might miss. It could help catch training data poisoning, backdoors, or undesirable knowledge acquired during pre-training, strengthening alignment and trust in deployed models.
Background
Overthinking originally referred to models generating excessive reasoning tokens; here it is repurposed to mean amplifying reasoning task vectors beyond normal levels. Task vectors represent directions in weight space that correspond to specific capabilities, obtained by subtracting the parameters of a pre-trained model from a fine-tuned model. In this work, the 'reasoning direction' is extracted from a reasoning-distilled model relative to its base instruct model, then amplified to force the model to think more openly, potentially revealing hidden knowledge.
Key Details
The key innovation is amplifying the difference between a reasoning model and a base model by a factor α > 1, instead of just interpolating. Layer-wise attenuation strategies were introduced to selectively boost reasoning without degrading output quality. The effectiveness varies by secret type: some require perturbation specifically along the reasoning direction, while any large weight perturbation works for others.

Key Details
