Background
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lets large language models pull information from external knowledge bases, but the retrieval step can become a latency bottleneck. Standard approaches rely on embedding models or lexical search; these may miss semantic nuance. Learned retrieval trains a model to choose retrieval actions, potentially improving both speed and relevance.
Key Details
Learned retrieval models often use a teacher-student setup; Spotify's Parallel Fusion Router trains a small router LLM with an LLM-as-judge reward function. Unlike traditional embedding similarity or keyword matching like grep, learned methods aim to select more relevant contexts while reducing retrieval overhead.
